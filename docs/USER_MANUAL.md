# 📖 PromptCraft: End-User Manual & Operation Guide

Welcome to **PromptCraft**! This user manual walks you through how to use PromptCraft to build, optimize, and learn generative AI prompts.

---

## 🚀 1. Getting Started

### Accessing the Application
1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Apple Safari).
2. Navigate to `http://localhost:5173` (or the deployed server address).
3. The PromptCraft **Home Dashboard** will load.

---

## 🛠️ 2. Using the Guided Prompt Builder

The Guided Builder is your primary tool for creating new prompts from scratch without staring at a blank page.

### Step-by-Step Workflow:
1. **Click "Prompt Builder"** in the top navigation bar.
2. **Select your task domain:**
   - Choose one of the category chips: *Writing & Content*, *Programming & Technical*, *Education & Learning*, *Research & Analysis*, *Creative & Artistic*, *Professional & Business*, or *Brainstorming*.
3. **Choose your detail mode:**
   - **Quick Mode (Default):** Displays 3 essential fields for fast prompt generation.
   - **Detailed Mode:** Displays all 6-7 fields including tone, constraints, and target audience. Click the **Detailed Mode** button in the header to switch.
4. **Fill in the guided fields:**
   - Type in your requirements. Notice the placeholder text gives you concrete examples.
   - Click **"Why this matters"** on any field to read an educational tip explaining how that parameter improves AI responses.
5. **Observe the Live Output Preview:**
   - The right-hand column assembles your prompt in real-time as you type.
   - The **Prompt Quality Analyzer** updates scores across Specificity, Context, Clarity, Structure, and Actionability.
6. **Save or Copy your prompt:**
   - Click the **Copy** button to copy the prompt to your clipboard.
   - Click the **Save** button to store the prompt in your local history for future reference.
   - Paste the copied prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI tool of your choice!

---

## ⚡ 3. Using the Prompt Improver

The Prompt Improver is designed for situations where you already have a draft prompt, but the AI is giving you generic or disappointing responses.

### Step-by-Step Workflow:
1. **Click "Prompt Improver"** in the top navigation.
2. **Paste your prompt** into the text area (or click one of the sample prompt chips like *Marketing Email* or *Study Aid* to try an example).
3. **Click "Improve Prompt & Explain Why"**:
   - The system analyzes your prompt's structural weaknesses.
   - An enhanced, production-ready version of your prompt is generated.
4. **Read the Explainable Breakdown:**
   - Review each itemized improvement. The system explains *what* was added, *why* it helps, and *which prompt pattern* was applied (such as the Persona Pattern or Output Automator).
5. **Copy the upgraded prompt** and use it in your preferred AI platform.

---

## 📚 4. Browsing Annotated Templates

1. **Click "Templates"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Filter templates by category (Education, Coding, Writing, etc.) or use the search bar.
3. On any template card:
   - Click **"Customize Template Variables"** to open the interactive parameter editor.
   - Type in your custom subjects or parameters; the template updates in real-time.
   - Read the inline **HCI Insights** explaining why each placeholder was included.
   - Click **"Copy Customized"** or **"Open in Builder"** to transfer the template into the guided builder form.

---

## 🎓 5. Exploring the Learning Hub

1. **Click "Learning Hub"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Explore the four educational sections:
   - **Prompt Anatomy:** Learn the 5 building blocks of every effective prompt (Role, Task, Context, Format, Constraints).
   - **Common Mistakes:** Review naive prompts side-by-side with corrected versions.
   - **Academic Patterns:** Study formal prompt engineering patterns from Vanderbilt University research.
   - **Quick Rules:** 12 easy rules you can memorize for everyday prompt writing.

---

## 🕒 6. Managing History & Saved Prompts

1. **Click "History"** in the top navigation bar.
2. View all prompts you've constructed or improved.
3. Filter by **Builder** or **Improver**.
4. Click **"Export JSON"** to download a backup file of your prompt library.
5. Click the trash icon to remove individual prompts, or click **"Clear All"** to reset your local history.
